PANDA DirectShift + Scharr + Epsilon-R3: S1 WV3 / S3 GF2, 48 hours each
Campaign: PANDA_S1_DIRECTSHIFT_SCHARR_R3_S13_48H_20261014_v1
Method: research_log/PANDA_Stage1_DirectShift_Scharr_EpsilonR3_MethodSpec_2026-10-14.md

Purpose and interpretation
  Freshly train the prior-free direct global PAN translation regressor jointly
  with the unchanged W112 d123 P0 Teacher. Do not reuse EdgeMatch/residual
  checkpoints. This is a registered hyperparameter search, not a performance
  result, and not a controlled claim that removing EdgeMatch alone caused a
  measured change: epsilon radius, support handling and fresh seeds also change.
  S1 uses WV3 (8 MS bands); S3 uses GF2 (4 MS bands). These are independent lanes
  with different data/targets, not server-reproducibility replicas. S2 is omitted.

Fixed method, data and evaluation contract
  Three strictly positive losses: L_rec + lambda_abs L_abs + lambda_epsilon L_eps.
  Direct u=(dy,dx); zero-initialized FC2; no matching, prior, prewarp, tanh, clamp,
  integer rounding, confidence gate, FR training, Stage 2 or KD.
  RR epsilon radius3, area-uniform disk r=3sqrt(U), dedicated RNG, PAN only,
  augmentation then epsilon, every update, native anchor detached only in L_eps.
  RGB mean then fixed Scharr absolute x/y channels; no edge normalization.
  Native LMS is separate from bicubic reconstruction base. Stored source registry
  and bands remain pinned. Both native/epsilon Aligner branches share one fixed
  support policy, defined and numerically tested by the DirectShift implementation
  and recorded in baseline/support manifests. No arbitrary data-dependent crop.
  Teacher W112 d123, PAN+BICUBIC_MS P0, effective RR batch48=12x4, AdamW betas
  (0.9,0.999), epsilon1e-8, weight_decay.01, teacher LR1e-4, aligner LR1e-5,
  100-update warmup then cosine to0, FP32, AMP/TF32 off. Existing paired spatial
  augmentation is retained identically for PAN/MS/LMS/GT: Hflip probability1.0,
  Vflip probability1.0, then uniform rot90. This means both flips always occur
  and only four rotational orientations are sampled; it is NOT a full D4 random
  augmentation policy. The unchanged choice is explicit, not an optimality claim.
  No checkpoint donor.
  Train/val64, official RR256 and FR512. Official evaluation is all20 RR and all20
  FR scenes from exactly one model state; original PAN, unmasked full images,
  unchanged metric implementation. HQNR/SCC/ERGAS are printed at every evaluation.
  H50 evaluation steps:1000, every2000 through50000, and25000 (27 total).
  Independent RNG streams for Aligner/Teacher initialization, RR sampling,
  augmentation and epsilon; diagnostic RNG does not consume training streams.

48-hour admission (independent measured wall clock per server)
  Wall clock starts before disposable preflight; one shared48h deadline.
  Preflight uses disposable state to verify gradient routing, initialization,
  warp signs/precision, fixed support, epsilon area distribution, data identities,
  all20+20 official evaluation and readback. Preflight weights are discarded.
  Measure update p90, full RR+FR evaluation and diagnostic times before admitting
  a run. An artifact cannot be exported before a real qualified state exists:
  until then, reserve300s per potentially qualifying evaluation as an explicitly
  unmeasured conservative estimate, not a claimed measurement. After qualified
  exports, keep max(300s, all observed artifact costs); likewise increase full
  evaluation and diagnostic reserves to observed maxima. Old EdgeMatch timings
  are NOT valid measured DirectShift ETAs.
  Raw ETA = remaining_updates*p90 + remaining_eval_count*eval_time + measured
  diagnostic cost + artifact reserve. Apply1.2 safety exactly once.
  Admission: elapsed +1.2*candidate_full_ETA +1.2*selected_recipe_ETA*
  remaining_confirmations +7200 closeout <=48h. Also require25K judgment point
  to fit. Reserve two confirmations during search; one forR2 admission, zeroR3.
  Search starts only before22h, optimizer followups before36h, no new run after46h.
  These are start gates, not permission to kill a live recipe at a phase boundary.
  Fixed2h closeout covers final evaluation, artifacts, queued uploads and summary.
  No H100/H150 extension in this campaign: preserve comparable H50 comparisons
  and confirmation capacity. Lack of capacity is recorded, not silently shortened.
  Conservative optional pruning only after25K with two preceding distinct
  same-seed/schedule recipes dominating at three common official evaluation steps
  and no recent target-gap progress. DF00 and every confirmation are protected.

Required diagnostic evidence
  Log unweighted/weighted losses and isolated Aligner/Teacher gradient norms,
  zero-init-to-early-layer gradient propagation, native shift distributions,
  support violations, epsilon bins<=1 /1..2 /2..3, and known response magnitudes
  .25/.5/1/2/3px using fixed train/val/RR/FR diagnostic IDs. Diagnostics never
  backpropagate FR training updates or alter selection/evaluation masks.
  Good RR synthetic response alone is not proof of FR geometric registration.
